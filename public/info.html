About Ciral

Ciral is a Pakistani online store selling clothing, footwear, electronics and home goods, delivered across Pakistan. Orders are tracked from Pending to In Route, Shipped and Delivered, and customers can message support from inside their account.

Terms

Prices are in PKR. Payment options (Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa) are selectable options only — no payment is taken on this website; payment details are confirmed with you after you place an order. Stock is checked at checkout. Products already used in an order cannot be deleted by sellers.

Privacy

We store only what you provide to shop: your name, phone, address, orders, wishlist, reviews and in-site messages. Passwords are stored only as PBKDF2 hashes. No email or SMS is sent by this site, so your messages stay inside Ciral. Product gallery images are generic category placeholders, not photos of the exact product.

FAQ

How do I track my order? Sign in on My Account → My Orders → Track & Details. You will see a timeline from Pending to Delivered.

How do I use a coupon? Enter WELCOME10 at checkout for 10% off.

Can I return an order? Message support from your order page with your order number and we will reply in-site.

Is my payment taken here? No. Choose a payment option at checkout and we confirm payment details with you afterwards.
